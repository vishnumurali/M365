Always verify dates

This tracker summarizes Microsoft announcements known as of 2026. Lifecycle dates can change, so confirm on Microsoft's Lifecycle site and your tenant's Message Center before committing plans.

Date Item Where Replace with Guide
Apr 2021 SharePoint 2010 end of support On-prem SE or SPO Upgrade paths
Aug–Nov 2020 SharePoint 2010 workflows SPO Power Automate Legacy → Power Automate
Apr 2023 SharePoint 2013 end of support On-prem SE or SPO On-prem to Online
2024 Stream (Classic) retired M365 Stream on SharePoint –
Apr 2024 2013 workflows off for new tenants SPO Power Automate –
Nov 2024 ACS/add-ins blocked for new tenants SPO SPFx + Entra apps –
2 Apr 2026 SharePoint Add-ins retired SPO SPFx, Azure, Entra apps PHA challenges & migration
2 Apr 2026 Azure ACS for SPO retired SPO Entra app registrations (certs, Sites.Selected) Sites.Selected
2 Apr 2026 SharePoint 2013 workflows retired SPO Power Automate / NAC Workflow migration
14 Jul 2026 SharePoint Server 2016 & 2019 end of support On-prem Subscription Edition or SPO Subscription Edition
14 Jul 2026 SharePoint Designer 2013 end of support Desktop Power Automate, Power Apps, formatting SharePoint Designer
Jul 2026 InfoPath 2013 end of support; Forms Services retiring in SPO Desktop / SPO Power Apps, NAC, SPFx InfoPath retirement
2025–2026 SharePoint alerts retirement (announced) SPO List rules, Power Automate Microsoft Lists

#Interview questions

Q1Which SharePoint Online retirements happened on 2 April 2026?

SharePoint Add-ins (SharePoint-hosted and provider-hosted), Azure ACS app authentication for SPO, and SharePoint 2013 workflows.

Q2What is the supported on-premises SharePoint version after July 2026?

SharePoint Server Subscription Edition. SharePoint 2016 and 2019 reached end of extended support on 14 July 2026.
