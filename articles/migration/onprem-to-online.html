#Interview questions

Q1What must be in place before you migrate content to SharePoint Online?

Identity synchronization (Entra Connect) so users and groups resolve, the target architecture (site mapping, hubs, templates), mapping files, rebuilt replacements for critical customizations (workflows, forms, web parts), a tested pilot, and a communication and support plan.

Q2How do you handle subsites when moving to SharePoint Online?

Evaluate each subsite. Significant subsites with their own audience or owners become separate site collections associated with a hub (flattening). Small, tightly coupled ones may stay as subsites in the new site, or merge into libraries. ShareGate can copy a subsite into a new site collection.
